The Home screen is your daily dashboard: today's doses, your progress, and the next dose that needs attention.

## Quick Steps

1. Check the Next Dose pill at the bottom of the screen first.
2. Review the progress ring and week bars for today's status.
3. Scroll down to the Not Taken and Taken lists to log doses.
4. Open the More tab for Help, Schedule Times, Settings, and About.

## Next Dose

![Image](MS_IPH_HomeView_Menu)

A pill above the tab bar always shows where you stand: the next dose that's due or overdue, the next upcoming dose if nothing is due yet, or "All doses taken today" once everything for the day is handled. The pill is informational only — tap it to see what's next, but mark a dose taken from the dose list below.

## Today's Doses

![Image](MS_IPH_HomeView_TodaysMedications)

Today's doses are listed in two sections: **Not Taken** (anything still due, late, or upcoming) and **Taken** (anything already logged), both ordered by time. Each row has its own **Mark Taken** button so you can see and change a dose's status at a glance, in addition to tapping the row or swiping it.
